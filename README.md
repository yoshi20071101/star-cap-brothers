## About

### A Classic Run, Made Simple

Many beginners love side-scrolling games but find them hard to start. This app gives a friendly, original one that runs right in the browser.

- Meet Yoshi-kun: a hard-working guy with a green star cap and mustache
- He runs through a sunny afternoon meadow with soft, round art
- Original look and characters, made with care
- Short and easy to pick up

### How the Game Works

Run right, jump over gaps, collect coins, and reach the goal flag before time runs out. There are three short stages.

- Move left and right, and jump on platforms or over gaps
- Grab a star to grow golden, jump higher, and defeat Mokomoko
- Stomp Mokomoko enemies, or lose a life if they touch you
- Goal brings fireworks and a bonus for the time you have left

### Play Anywhere, Grow Next

The game works with a keyboard or on-screen phone buttons, and it switches between Japanese and English.

- Keyboard: arrow keys, Space, or W, A, D
- Phone: big on-screen buttons for left, right, and jump
- Your best score is saved on your device
- Next: more stages and new enemies, built step by step
